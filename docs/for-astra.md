# Cosmic Weather: Astra's guide

## What it is

A daily page for your clients and community. Each morning, top to bottom: a greeting and tonight's sky (moon phase and a stargazing rating), live weather for home and up to three other places, a 1 to 5 check-in with a journal prompt, their living journal, a short Human Design brief for the day explained in plain words, and their own chart. Every screen points back to you.

## How people get in

- They open the link on their phone or computer.
- They sign in with **their Google account** or **their email** (we send a sign-in link, so there is no password to remember).
- The first time, they enter their first name, birth date, time and place, and their home town. The app calculates their chart from that. If they have no birth time, they or you can fill in the design by hand.
- On a phone they can add it to their home screen so it opens like an app.

## Your Studio

You see one extra tab that clients do not: **Studio**.

- **Your people**: everyone who has signed in, with their birth details.
- **Their design**: set or correct a person's type, profile, authority and defined centers. Their daily page updates right away.
- **Add a session recap**: date, title, what you covered, and action steps (one per line). The recap shows on their Sessions tab and each action step lands in their to-do list marked "1:1".

## What you can shape

Everything a client reads is yours to rewrite. The starter wording is placeholder.

| Piece | How many | Example of what's there now |
|---|---|---|
| One line per center, for when the Sun is there | 9 | "Words want out today." (Throat) |
| One line per type | 5 | "Wait for the invitation." (Projector) |
| Lines for defined vs open centers | 2 | "This center is defined in your chart, so it lands on home ground." |
| Journal prompts per moon phase | 8 phases, 2 or more each | "What are you ready to put down?" (last quarter) |
| Forecast headline per moon phase | 8 | "Bright and building" (waxing gibbous) |
| Evening check-in words | 5 | Clear, Breezy, Foggy, Stormy, Still |

Write as many prompts per phase as you like. More prompts means less repeating.

## How to give feedback

- **Look and layout**: comment on the mockup, or just tell JC.
- **Wording**: send your versions of the lines above in any form. A doc, a voice note, a list.
